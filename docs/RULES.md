# Uncle Mike's NFL Pick Em — Rules

Confirmed with Dom on 2026-11-12. The weekly sheet and standings for the 2026 season were the reference.

## Weekly pick-em

- Pick a winner for every game of the week and assign each game a unique confidence value from 1 to N, where N is the number of games that week. No value can be used twice.
- A correct pick earns its confidence value and shows green. A wrong pick earns 0 and shows red.
- **Lock:** every pick for the week locks at kickoff of the first game, usually Thursday night. Nothing can change after that, Sunday games included. The lock time is a per-pool setting and this is its default.
- **Late reminders:** members who haven't submitted get reminder emails before the lock.
- **Missed week:** a member who doesn't submit gets the lowest weekly score among those who did, minus 1.
- **Weekly tiebreaker:** guess the total points scored in the Monday night game. Closest guess wins.
  - If there are two Monday games, use the later one.
  - Week 18 has no Monday game, so use the last game of the week.
- **Canceled game:** taken off the week, so the top confidence value that week is lower. This is an organizer setting.
- **Picks are hidden** from other members until the lock. After the lock the whole group can see them.
- **Late joiners:** get the missed-week score for each week before they joined.
- **Weekly ties:** members tied on points share the place, shown as "1*" in the standings.

## Game of the Week (GOW)

- One game each week is the GOW.
- **Scoring:**
  - The weekly score doesn't change: a right GOW pick earns its confidence value and a wrong one earns 0.
  - On the yearly score, a right GOW pick counts double. Example: 15 confidence gives 15 weekly and 30 yearly.
  - On the yearly score, a wrong GOW pick counts negative. Example: 15 confidence gives 0 weekly and −15 yearly for that week.
- **Missed week:** no separate GOW penalty. A non-submitter's yearly score for that week is the lowest yearly score among submitters (GOW doubling and negatives included) minus 1. Every non-submitter gets that same score.
- **Season tiebreaker:** total GOW points (doubles and negatives included).
- **Choosing the GOW:**
  - Before each week, the admin (the group's creator and organizer) gets an email listing the three most-hyped games, each with a one-click button to choose it, plus a link to choose any other game.
  - The organizer must choose by Tuesday noon ET. If they haven't, the app picks the most-hyped game, judged by national TV slot, spread and total, and ESPN's matchup rating.
  - Picks open only after the GOW is set, so nobody picks before knowing which game it is.

## Death pool (survivor)

- Pick one team per week. If the team wins, the entry survives. Each team can be used once per season.
- One person can have several entries (e.g. "Name 1" through "Name 4"), and each is paid for separately.
- If every remaining entry is eliminated in the same week, all of them come back.
- The pool runs until one entry is left. If more than one is still alive after Week 18, they split the pot.
- The death pool has its own pot, and its fee is set separately.
- **Missed week:** a missed pick eliminates the entry.
- **NFL tie game:** counts as a loss.
- **Second life (optional):** the organizer can give each entry one extra life, and can only turn this on before Week 1. The first loss uses it up and the second loss eliminates the entry.
  - A missed pick uses up a life, the same as a loss.
  - If every remaining entry loses in the same week, each entry that still has a life just loses it and stays in.
  - The revive rule only applies when every remaining entry was already on its last life. In that case they all come back.

## Money

- The pool owner sets the fees (weekly, yearly, death pool), the payment methods (Venmo, Zelle, Cash App, and so on) and the handles.
- Each member has an entry type. **Weekly** plays for the weekly pot only and pays the weekly fee. **Season** plays for the weekly and yearly pots and pays both fees. The member picks one when joining, and the organizer can change it.
- The pool owner marks who has paid. The app never moves money.
- The pool owner sets how the pots are split between weekly winners and yearly winners.

## Groups and notifications

- Sign in with Google. Users create groups and invite people.
- Automated, styled HTML emails go out for: week results, winning a week, elimination from the death pool, late-pick reminders, and the admin's GOW choice.

## Organizer settings

These are the defaults. The group organizer can change them per group:

- **Tied NFL game, pick-em:** 0 points for everyone.
- **Tied NFL game, death pool:** counts as a loss.
- **Death pool second life:** off.
- **Lock time:** kickoff of the week's first game. The death pool uses the same lock.
- **Rule changes:** allowed mid-season. A change only applies to weeks that haven't started.
- **Fees, pot splits and payment methods:** set by the organizer, as described under Money.

## Analytics (after lock)

- The chance of each member winning the week and the year, updated after each game and each week.
- A rooting guide showing which result in each remaining game gives each member the best chance to win.
- Breakdowns of how the group picked.
